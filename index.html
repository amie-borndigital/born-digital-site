The Problem

The leak nobody's looking for

Every business owner knows their sales numbers. Revenue this month, deals won, the top line. Almost none of them know what's happening underneath it: the leads that went quiet, the quotes nobody chased, the customers who never heard from you again after they signed.

A lead comes in. Someone gets a quote. Then, nothing. An existing customer stops booking, or never hears from you again after the sale. Not because your product's wrong, or your price is off, or your ads aren't working, but because nobody followed up fast enough, or nobody ever went back at all.

You can be hitting your numbers and still be leaking thousands a month in deals nobody chased. Most business owners have never actually looked at what's happening inside the pipeline itself, only at what comes out the other end.
