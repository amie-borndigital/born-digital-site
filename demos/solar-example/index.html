Homes and small businesses

Power the house, the shop or the workshop.

Whether it’s a family home or a small business, we start by looking at how you use energy, then suggest the mix of solar, heat pump and storage that suits the building. If something won’t work for your property, we’ll say so.

  • A survey of your roof, loft, heating and fuse board before we quote
  • Systems sized to the building and the energy you actually use
  • Solar with or without a battery, which you can add now or later
  • Network operator paperwork handled for you

Grant and funding schemes for heat pumps, solar and batteries change often. We check what’s open when we quote and explain what your property may be eligible for.

How it works

From first chat to switch‑on in four steps.

  1. 01

    Tell us about your property

    Ring us or send an email with your postcode and a few details. Photos of the roof and fuse board help us prepare.

  2. 02

    We visit and survey

    We look at the roof, the loft, the heating and the fuse board, and talk through how you use energy day to day.

  3. 03

    A written quote

    You get a quote in writing that sets out what we’d fit and why, with no pressure to go ahead.

  4. 04

    We install and hand over

    We fit everything on the days we agreed, switch it on and show you how it all works before we leave.
